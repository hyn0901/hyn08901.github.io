About Guangzhou

A major southern city with deep cultural roots

Located in Guangdong Province, Guangzhou has long played an important role in trade, transportation, migration, and cultural exchange. Today, it remains a city where tradition and innovation live comfortably side by side.

Must-Visit Places

Four memorable places to experience Guangzhou

Canton Tower

Canton Tower is one of the most iconic landmarks in Guangzhou. Its elegant shape and panoramic views represent the city's confident modern image.

Pearl River

The Pearl River brings softness and beauty to the city. Evening cruises and riverside walks offer some of Guangzhou's most memorable views.

Shamian Island

Shamian Island is calm, historic, and visually charming. Its leafy streets and heritage buildings offer a gentle contrast to the city's busy pace.

Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

This celebrated cultural site highlights Lingnan craftsmanship through fine carvings, ceramics, and beautiful traditional architectural detail.

Food in Guangzhou

A city loved for its Cantonese flavors

Guangzhou is widely known as one of the best food cities in China. Its dining culture values freshness, balance, care, and a refined sense of taste.

Dim Sum

Small dishes shared with tea, dim sum reflects the social warmth and delicate flavors of Guangzhou's food culture.

Char Siu

Sweet, savory, and beautifully roasted, char siu is one of the city's most recognizable and comforting dishes.

Wonton Noodles

This classic dish combines delicate noodles, clear broth, and tender wontons in a simple but memorable form.

Double-Skin Milk

Smooth and lightly sweet, this dessert shows the gentle elegance that often defines Cantonese taste.

Culture and Lifestyle

Relaxed, refined, and full of everyday warmth

Guangzhou has a lifestyle that feels lively without being rushed. Morning tea, neighborhood markets, riverside evenings, shaded streets, and shared meals all help define the city's gentle rhythm.

Lingnan culture remains visible in architecture, language, crafts, gardens, and food. This gives Guangzhou a strong local identity even as it continues to grow as a modern international city.

Lingnan Heritage

Traditional design, decorative detail, and regional character remain highly visible.

Tea and Food Culture

Sharing meals and enjoying tea are essential parts of social life in Guangzhou.

Urban Vitality

The city feels efficient, international, and energetic while still staying welcoming.

Why You Should Visit Guangzhou

A city that keeps rewarding curiosity

Guangzhou is an ideal destination for anyone interested in Chinese city life, regional culture, architecture, and food. It is visually attractive, culturally meaningful, and easy to present as a city of both tradition and progress.
